# Jaundice

> Source: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/jaundice/

**Jaundice is when your skin or the whites of your eyes turn yellow. It can be a sign of something serious, such as liver disease, so you need to get urgent medical help.**

> **⚠️ Urgent advice:**
>
> - your skin or the white part of your eyes look yellow
>
> You may also have itchy skin, darker pee and paler poo than usual.
>
> You can call 111 or [get help from 111 online](https://111.nhs.uk/triage/check-your-symptoms).

## Symptoms of jaundice

![A white hand with skin looking yellow next to a white hand with no skin changes for comparison.](https://assets.nhs.uk/nhsuk-cms/images/S_1117_jaundice-hand_C0016614.original.width-320.jpg)

Your skin may look yellow if you have jaundice.

![An eye, on someone with white skin, with a blue iris. The white part of the eye has turned yellow. The yellow is darker at the corners of the eye.](https://assets.nhs.uk/nhsuk-cms/images/S_1117_jaundice-eye_M1900084.original.width-320.jpg)

The white part of your eyes may also look yellow.

![Someone with brown skin who has yellowing of the white part of the eye and the skin under the eyebrows.](https://assets.nhs.uk/nhsuk-cms/images/shutterstock_1343303249.width-320.jpg)

Yellowing of the skin may be less noticeable if you have brown or black skin, but the white part of your eyes will look yellow.

## Causes of jaundice

You may get jaundice if a substance called bilirubin builds up in your body.

There are lots of possible reasons for this and some of them are serious.

They include:

- [alcohol-related liver disease](/conditions/alcohol-related-liver-disease-arld/)
- [gallstones](/conditions/gallstones/)
- [hepatitis](/conditions/hepatitis/)
- [pancreatitis](/conditions/acute-pancreatitis/)
- [sickle cell disease](/conditions/sickle-cell-disease/)

Treatments for jaundice depend on what's causing it.
