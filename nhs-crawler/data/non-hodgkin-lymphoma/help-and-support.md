# Help and support for non-Hodgkin lymphoma

> Source: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/non-hodgkin-lymphoma/help-and-support/

## Where to find help and support for non-Hodgkin lymphoma

If you have non-Hodgkin lymphoma, your specialist treatment team will be there to support you throughout your treatment.

They will also be able to give you information about local support services you may find helpful.

There are also national cancer charities that can offer support and information about non-Hodgkin lymphoma.

### Blood Cancer UK

Information and support for anyone affected by non-Hodgkin lymphoma.

- Website: [www.bloodcancer.org.uk](https://bloodcancer.org.uk/)
- Helpline: 0808 208 0888
- [Blood Cancer UK: online community forum](https://forum.bloodcancer.org.uk/)
