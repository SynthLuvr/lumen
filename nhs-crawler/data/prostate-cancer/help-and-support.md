# Help and support for prostate cancer

> Source: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/prostate-cancer/help-and-support/

## Where to find help and support for prostate cancer

If you have prostate cancer, your specialist treatment team will be there to support you throughout your treatment. They will also be able to give you information about local support services.

There are also national cancer charities that offer support and information about prostate cancer.

### **Prostate Cancer UK**

Information and support for anyone affected by prostate cancer.

- Website: [prostatecanceruk.org](https://prostatecanceruk.org/)
- [Prostate Cancer UK: information and support](https://prostatecanceruk.org/prostate-information-and-support/get-support)
- [Prostate Cancer UK: contact a specialist nurse](https://prostatecanceruk.org/prostate-information-and-support/get-support/our-specialist-nurses)
- [Prostate Cancer UK's online community](https://community.prostatecanceruk.org/).
