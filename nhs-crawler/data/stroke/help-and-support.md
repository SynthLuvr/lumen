# Support after a stroke

> Source: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/stroke/help-and-support/

## Getting help after a stroke

Having a stroke can affect your mental health. It can help to talk about how you're feeling.

You can get talking therapies for free on the NHS. These services can help you find ways to cope.

Help is available in person, by video, over the phone or as an online course.

[Find an NHS talking therapies service](/nhs-services/mental-health-services/find-nhs-talking-therapies-for-anxiety-and-depression/)

### Support from charities

You can get advice from charities such as:

- [Different Strokes](https://differentstrokes.co.uk/what-we-do/)
- [Stroke Association](https://www.stroke.org.uk/stroke/support)
- [Brain and Spine Foundation: join a support group](https://www.brainandspine.org.uk/support-for-you/peer-support-groups/)
