# What is vulval cancer?

> Source: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/vulval-cancer/what-is-vulval-cancer/

![Diagram of the genitals with labels showing parts of the vulva: the clitoris, openings to the vagina and urethra, and inner and outer labia.](https://assets.nhs.uk/nhsuk-cms/images/VID_1857_-_Vulval_Cancer_E5F2zso.width-320.jpg)

- Vulval cancer (also called vulvar cancer) is cancer that's found anywhere in the vulva.
- The vulva is the area around the opening of the vagina, including the inner and outer lips around the vagina (labia) and the clitoris.
- Vulval cancer is rare and mostly affects women over 65 years old.
- It can affect anyone who has a vulva.
- Vulval cancer may be caused by certain types of [human papillomavirus (HPV)](/conditions/human-papilloma-virus-hpv/), or by skin conditions that affect your vulva.
- How serious it is depends on how big it is, if it has spread and your general health.
