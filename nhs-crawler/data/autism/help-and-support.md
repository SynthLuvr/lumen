# Help and support for autistic people and their families

> Source: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/autism/help-and-support/

## Where to find help and support

If you’re autistic or caring for an autistic child or adult, you can get support from:

- a GP or local autism team
- your local council
- your school, college or workplace
- local support services
- national charities

> **💡 Information:** Find support in your area Autism services directory – National Autistic Society Find local information, advice and support (IAS) services – Council for disabled children
